Your online proof of work: the kiosk

Every seller, fundi, rider and business can open a free kiosk with a business photo, logo, about text, areas covered, charges and photos of past work. KaziApa adds an automatic track record: completed orders, items sold, ratings, Nitume jobs and testimonials from people who really dealt with you.

Questions

Is KaziApa free?

Joining, browsing, posting and chatting are free. Optional extras such as boosts and unlocking a landlord's contact cost a small fee paid by M-Pesa.

Where can I use KaziApa?

On Android from Google Play, or in your browser at kaziapa.co.ke.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile in the app and choose Delete my account, or visit kaziapa.co.ke/delete-account.html.
