Find jobs and gigs near you

KaziApa Jobs works two ways: employers and neighbours post work they need done, and job seekers post that they are available for work. Everyone talks directly in the app.

Download on Google Play

Two ways to use KaziApa Jobs

I need someoneYou are hiring. Post the job, the pay and the place, and people nearby can respond.
I offer my workYou are looking for work. Post your skills and availability so people can contact you.

How it works

  1. Choose I need or I offer.
  2. Describe the work or your skills, the area and the pay.
  3. People near you see your post and message you in the app.
  4. Agree the details before work starts.

Build trust with a kiosk

A free KaziApa kiosk is your online proof of work. Add your photo, years of experience, areas you cover, your charges, photos of past jobs and testimonials from customers. Share the link on WhatsApp when you apply or look for gigs.

Safety tips for job seekers

  • Never pay money to get a job.
  • Meet in public places and tell someone where you are going.
  • Agree the pay and the work before you start.
  • Report suspicious posts in the app.

Questions

Is KaziApa Jobs free?

Yes. Posting a job or your availability is free. You can boost a post to be seen first.

What kind of work can I find?

Casual work, vibarua, short gigs and skilled work, depending on what people near you post.

Can I hire a fundi instead?

Yes. Use the Services section or Find a Pro to see fundis with their work photos and ratings.

Need work or need workers?

Download KaziApa to post a job or offer your skills.
