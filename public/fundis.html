How to choose a fundi with confidence

  1. Open Services or Find a Pro and choose your town and the type of fundi.
  2. Open their kiosk: work photos, years of experience, areas they cover and charges.
  3. Check the track record: completed jobs, ratings and testimonials from people who really dealt with them.
  4. Chat or call, agree the price and the job, then rate them afterwards.

For fundis: get more customers

Open a free kiosk with your photo, what you do, the areas you cover and your charges. Add photos of jobs you have done, including before and after pictures. Share your kiosk link on WhatsApp. Every completed job and every testimonial strengthens your proof of work, so new customers trust you faster.

Tips for hiring a fundi

  • Agree the price and what is included before work starts.
  • Pay a small deposit only, and the balance when the job is done.
  • Ask to see photos of similar jobs.
  • Report anyone who takes money and does not do the work.

Questions

Is it free to find a fundi?

Yes. Browsing fundis and contacting them is free.

Can I see a fundi's past work?

Yes. Fundis can add photos of completed jobs, with before and after pictures, to their kiosk.

I am a fundi. How do I join?

Download KaziApa, sign up with your phone number, and open My Kiosk in your profile to set up your page.
