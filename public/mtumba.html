Buy and sell mtumba in Kenya

KaziApa has a Mitumba marketplace for secondhand clothes. Buyers find items from sellers near them, and sellers and bale dealers get a kiosk that shows their stock, ratings and track record.

Download on Google Play

For buyers

  • Browse mitumba from sellers in your town and chat with them directly.
  • Look at the seller's kiosk: items, completed orders, ratings and testimonials.
  • Ask for clear photos of the actual items before you pay.
  • Use Find a Pro or Nitume if you want someone to source or collect items for you.

For sellers and bale dealers

Post your items and bales with photos and prices. Open a free kiosk with your business photo, your story and years of experience. KaziApa builds your track record automatically from real orders and ratings, so new customers can trust you before they message you. Share your kiosk link on WhatsApp and your shop poster.

Tips for buying mtumba safely

  • Ask about the grade and the condition, and how many pieces are in a bale.
  • Ask for photos or a short video of the actual stock.
  • Prefer sellers with ratings and testimonials.
  • Meet in a public place or pay on delivery where possible.
  • Report sellers who do not deliver what they advertised.

Boost your listing

Want to be seen first? A boost puts your listing at the top. Boosts start from Ksh 30 and are paid by M-Pesa.

Questions

What is mtumba?

Mtumba (also written mitumba) means secondhand clothes, often sold in bales or by the piece.

Is it free to sell?

Posting is free. Optional extras such as boosting a listing cost a small fee.

Can I sell bales, not just single items?

Yes. Many sellers list bales, and you can describe the grade, count and price in your listing.
