Special Request: tell us what you need

Cannot find it in the app? Describe the item or service you need, with your budget and town. KaziApa will contact you, usually within 3 to 6 hours, to help you find it.

Download on Google Play

What is a Special Request?

Think of it as a personal shopper or concierge for things that are hard to find. Instead of scrolling through listings, you tell KaziApa what you need, and we come back to you.

How to send one

  1. Open Browse in the app and choose the Special Request tab.
  2. Describe what you need, for example: “Newborn socks size 0 to 3 months, budget Ksh 300”.
  3. Add your town.
  4. Send it. KaziApa will contact you within about 3 to 6 hours.

What can I ask for?

ItemsClothes, baby items, electronics, furniture and more.
Mitumba findsA specific size, grade or brand. See the <a href='/mtumba.html'>Mtumba page</a>.
ServicesA fundi, driver or helper. You can also use Find a Pro or <a href='/nitume.html'>Nitume</a>.
Anything localIf it is legal and you cannot find it, ask.

Tips for a good request

  • Say exactly what you need: size, colour, quantity.
  • Give your budget so we find the right options.
  • Name your town so we search near you.
  • Keep your phone on so we can reach you.

Questions

Is a Special Request free?

Sending a request is free. Any item or service you decide to buy is paid for as agreed with the seller.

How fast will I hear back?

KaziApa aims to contact you within about 3 to 6 hours.

What if you cannot find it?

We will tell you, and you can also post it on the Requests board so sellers can respond.
