My Market: your local market, online

My Market brings Kenyan markets onto your phone. Find the market near you, see who sells there, read market notices and list your own goods where your customers already shop.

Download on Google Play

What is My Market?

A My Market page is the online home of a real market. It shows the sellers and kiosks that trade there and the notices shared by the community. You can follow the markets you use so they appear in My Markets, and find Markets near you by distance.

What can I do in a market page?

Find sellersBrowse the directory of kiosks and sellers in that market, with their ratings and work history.
Read noticesSee updates shared for that market.
List your stockTag your listings to your market so shoppers in that market find you.
Suggest a marketCannot find yours? Suggest it and it can be added.

New to a market? Use a Market Guide

Some KaziApa members are Nitume Verified Market Guides. They help newcomers find sellers and shop in a market. See the Nitume page to hire one or become one.

For sellers and traders

Open a free kiosk, add photos of your stock and tag your listings to your market. Your kiosk builds a track record from real orders, ratings and testimonials, so shoppers can trust you before they visit your stall.

Questions

Is My Market free?

Yes. Finding markets, following them and reading notices is free.

Which markets are on KaziApa?

Markets across Kenya. You can search by name, see markets near you, and suggest one that is missing.

What is the difference between My Market and My Hood?

My Market is for places where people trade. My Hood is for where you live: your estate, apartment or hostel.
