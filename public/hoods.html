My Hood: your estate, apartment or hostel community

My Hood is the neighbourhood part of KaziApa. Join the community where you live to see local notices, find what your neighbours sell, and offer your own services close to home.

Download on Google Play

What is a Hood?

A Hood is a community for where you live: an estate or area, a town, a county, or a specific apartment or hostel. Search for yours, join it, and set it as your Hood. Its page opens on local notices.

What can I do in my Hood?

Read local noticesUpdates from people in your community.
Trade nearbyFind items and services from neighbours.
Offer servicesFundis, cleaners and small businesses can reach people next door.
Suggest your placeYour estate or building not listed? Suggest it.

Apartments and hostels are members only

Apartment and hostel communities are private. Their notices, listings and requests are visible only to members, and the person who created the place can remove members. Public estates and towns are open to everyone.

For landlords, caretakers and residents

Use your building's community for notices such as water, power or security updates, and to help neighbours trade safely. If you manage rentals, the Rent Tracker helps you record rent between you and your tenants.

Questions

Is My Hood free?

Yes. Joining a Hood and reading notices is free.

Can anyone see my apartment's notices?

No. Apartment and hostel communities are visible to members only.

Can I add my estate or building?

Yes. Suggest it in the app. Suggested places are reviewed before they appear.
