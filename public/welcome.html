What is KaziApa?

KaziApa is a free Kenyan app that brings your estate, market and town together in one place. See what is for sale, who is hiring, which fundi is nearby and which house is available, then chat with the person directly. Sellers and service providers get their own kiosk that shows their work, ratings and reviews, so people can trust them before they call.

Who should use KaziApa?

  • Buyers and shoppers who want to find good deals close to home.
  • Sellers and small businesses, from mtumba traders to shops and salons.
  • Fundis, drivers and service providers who want more customers and proof of their work.
  • Job seekers and employers looking for gigs and staff nearby.
  • Tenants, landlords and agents looking for or managing houses.
  • Estates, hostels and markets that want one place for notices and local trade.

How do I start?

  1. Download KaziApa from Google Play, or open kaziapa.co.ke in your browser.
  2. Sign up with your phone number. You get a code by SMS. No password to remember.
  3. Browse, post or chat. Find what you need near you, or post what you sell or the work you do.

How do I advertise my business?

Create your free KaziApa kiosk. Add your business photo and logo, what you sell or do, the areas you cover, your prices and photos of your best work. KaziApa builds your track record automatically from real orders, ratings and testimonials. Then share your kiosk link on WhatsApp, Facebook or your shop poster so customers can see your work before they contact you.

Want to be seen first? Boost a listing so it appears at the top of the app. Boosts start from Ksh 30 and are paid by M-Pesa.

Questions

Is KaziApa free?

Yes. Signing up, browsing, posting and chatting are free. Optional extras, such as boosting a listing or unlocking a landlord's contact, cost a small fee paid by M-Pesa.

Which areas does KaziApa cover?

KaziApa is built for Kenya, with markets, estates and towns across the country, including Nairobi and Nakuru.

How do I stay safe when buying or renting?

Chat inside the app first, meet in public places, look at a seller's ratings and testimonials, and never pay a deposit before you have seen the item or house. Report suspicious listings in the app.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile in the app and choose Delete my account, or visit kaziapa.co.ke/delete-account.html.
