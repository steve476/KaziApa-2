Your free kiosk: proof of your work, online

A KaziApa kiosk is your business page for sellers, shops, fundis, riders and service providers. It shows who you are, what you have done and what customers say about you.

Download on Google Play

What goes in a kiosk?

Business photo and logoShow your shop front, stall, vehicle or your work.
Your storyWhy customers should choose you.
What best describes youShop, transport, worker or service.
ExperienceYears of experience in your trade.
Areas you coverThe estates and towns you serve.
Your chargesPrices or ranges, in your own words.
Work photosJobs you have done, with before and after pictures.
Your itemsEverything you list for sale in one place.

A track record nobody can fake

KaziApa builds your track record automatically from real activity: orders completed, items sold, ratings, Nitume jobs done and how long you have been on KaziApa. Customers who have chatted or traded with you can leave a testimonial. You can hide one you do not want to show, but you cannot write your own.

Your dashboard

  • How many people viewed your kiosk this week, compared with last week.
  • Sales today and orders waiting for your confirmation.
  • A profile strength score with tips on what to add next.

Share it anywhere

Every kiosk has its own link. Put it in your WhatsApp status, on your poster, in your Facebook page, or send it to a customer who asks for references. Customers can also find you in Find a Pro, and on the public kiosks page where good kiosks are listed so people searching on Google can find them.

How to open yours

  1. Download KaziApa and sign up with your phone number.
  2. Open Profile and choose My Kiosk.
  3. Add your business photo, logo, story, experience, areas and charges.
  4. Post your items or add photos of your work, then tap Share My Kiosk.

Questions

Is the kiosk free?

Yes. Opening a kiosk is free.

Can anyone leave a testimonial?

No. Only people who have chatted or traded with you on KaziApa can leave one, so testimonials are real.

Does the kiosk show my phone number?

The kiosk link uses your kiosk name, not your phone number. Customers contact you through KaziApa chat.
