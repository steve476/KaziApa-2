How to use it

  1. Open Transport in KaziApa and choose your town.
  2. Pick the type of vehicle you need.
  3. Check the provider's profile and kiosk for ratings and completed work.
  4. Call or WhatsApp them directly to agree the trip and price.

For riders and drivers

List your vehicle, the area you serve and when you are available. Create a free kiosk to show your experience and testimonials from customers. For errands and deliveries, you can also become a Nitume provider.

Travel safely

  • Note the number plate and share it with someone you trust.
  • Agree the price before the trip starts.
  • Prefer providers with ratings and testimonials.
  • Report unsafe or fake listings.

Questions

Is there a booking fee?

No. You contact providers directly and agree the trip between you.

Can I find a lorry or pickup for moving?

Yes. Choose Lorry or Pickup in Transport and contact providers in your town.

Can I list my vehicle?

Yes. Post your vehicle type, your area and your availability in the app.
