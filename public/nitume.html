Nitume: send someone to run your errand

Nitume is KaziApa's errand and shopping service. Post what you need done, and a person near you can take the job. Or sign up as a Nitume provider and earn by helping others.

Download on Google Play

What does Nitume mean?

Nitume is Swahili for “send me”. On KaziApa it is the place where people who need something done connect with people nearby who are ready to do it: errand runners, shoppers, market guides and creative helpers.

What kind of errands can I post?

Shopping errandAsk someone to buy and bring items from a shop or market.
Fashion sourcingGet help finding clothes, mitumba or specific items.
Photography and videographyFind someone nearby to take photos or video.
General errandPick up, drop off, queue, or deliver something.
Market guideA local who helps newcomers find sellers and shop in a market.
OtherAnything else, described in your own words.

How to hire someone on Nitume

  1. Post a request. Say what you need, where, and the fee you are offering.
  2. A provider takes the job. Someone nearby accepts it, or you can send the request straight to a provider you trust.
  3. Chat and agree the details inside the app.
  4. Confirm when it is done. The job is confirmed in the app, so there is a record that the work was completed.

How to earn as a Nitume provider

If you are a shopper, boda rider, runner or helper, switch on Available for Nitume jobs in your profile. Customers posting requests near you will see you. Every completed job counts towards your KaziApa kiosk track record, and customers can leave testimonials about your work, so your reputation grows with every job done well.

Staying safe

  • Agree the task, the fee and where to meet before work starts.
  • Check the provider's kiosk: completed jobs, ratings and testimonials.
  • Keep chatting inside KaziApa so there is a record.
  • Report anyone who asks you to pay outside the agreed arrangement.

Questions

Is Nitume free?

You can join and post for free. Fees for the errand itself are agreed between you and the provider in the app, and any KaziApa fee is shown before you pay.

Where does Nitume work?

Nitume is built for Kenya. You see requests and providers near your town.

How do I become a Nitume provider?

Open Profile in the app and turn on Available for Nitume jobs. You can also create a free kiosk so customers can see your work.
