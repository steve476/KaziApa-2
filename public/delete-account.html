Delete your KaziApa account

KaziApa is operated by Kazinasi Technologies, Nairobi, Kenya. You can delete your account and data at any time.

Option 1: inside the app

  1. Open KaziApa (kaziapa.co.ke or the app) and log in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account, then type DELETE to confirm.

Option 2: by email

Email hello@kaziapa.co.ke from the address you registered with, or tell us the phone number on your account. Put "Delete my account" in the subject. We delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, profile photo and login details
  • Your listings, statuses, kiosk, requests and replies
  • Your chats and notifications
  • Your Rent Tracker data, including uploaded documents (landlords must first end active tenancies)
  • Your push notification subscriptions and blocked-user list

What is kept

  • Order and payment records are kept for accounting and for the other party, with your phone number removed.
  • Rent records of a landlord you rented from stay with that landlord, with your phone number removed.
  • Photos you sent inside chats may remain in storage for a short time but are no longer linked to you.
