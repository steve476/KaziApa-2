Land for sale

Browse plots, farm land, acres, half and quarter acres, and residential or commercial land. Contact sellers directly in the app.

  • Always visit the land before you pay anything.
  • Check the ownership documents and do an official search at the lands registry.
  • Do not pay deposits in cash without a signed agreement.
  • Report listings that look false.

For farmers and suppliers

Post your produce, land or equipment with photos and a price. Open a free kiosk to show your farm, years of experience and customer testimonials. A track record of real orders and ratings helps buyers trust you.

Questions

Is it free to list farm produce?

Yes. Posting is free. You can boost a listing to be seen first, from Ksh 30.

Can I find a tractor for hire?

Yes. Look under farm equipment and choose Tractor hire, or post a request for what you need.

Can I sell land on KaziApa?

You can list plots and land for sale. Buyers should always verify documents officially before paying.
