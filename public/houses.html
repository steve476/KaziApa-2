How house hunting works on KaziApa

  1. Browse listings in your town and open the ones you like.
  2. Unlock the verified contact of the agent with a one-time fee of Ksh 50 (cheaper than a wasted trip to a fake listing).
  3. Book a viewing. You pay Ksh 150 directly to the agent once they confirm a time.
  4. Rate the viewing afterwards. Fake listings are reported and the agent receives a strike.

For landlords and agents

Post your vacant houses with photos and rent. Landlords can also use the Rent Tracker to record who has paid. Tenants submit their rent payments, you confirm or dispute them, and KaziApa never touches your rent money.

Tips for renting safely

  • See the house before paying any deposit.
  • Be careful if a listing is much cheaper than similar houses nearby.
  • Ask for the agent's name and check their ratings.
  • Report fake or misleading listings in the app.

Questions

Is it free to browse houses?

Yes. Browsing is free. A small one-time fee unlocks a verified agent's contact, and viewings are paid directly to the agent.

Does KaziApa take my rent?

No. The Rent Tracker only records payments between tenants and landlords. KaziApa does not hold or touch rent money.

What if a house is fake?

Report it in the app. Agents of reported fake listings receive strikes.
